Policy & Operating Environment

LAKE STATES — WISCONSIN

Wisconsin board takes up the rewrite path for forestry grant and state-aid rules today

The Wisconsin Natural Resources Board meets at 8:30 a.m. today in Sturgeon Bay with Board Order FR-02-26 on the agenda. The requested action is to approve a statement of scope for changes to Chapter NR 47, which governs forestry grant and state-aid administration, and to conditionally approve a future public hearing and review by the Legislative Council Rules Clearinghouse.

This is the start of a rulemaking process, not a final rule change. For counties, fire departments, private forest landowners and others using forestry-assistance programs, the practical point is that the administrative framework is now moving toward revision; eligibility, reimbursement and program-specific effects should not be assumed until proposed language is published.

NATIONAL — WILDFIRE OPERATIONS

National fire preparedness drops to Level 2 as resource ordering eases

The national wildland fire Preparedness Level was lowered to Level 2 Tuesday morning. The Forest Service says initial attack and wildfire activity have decreased significantly, resource availability is adequate, and national ordering through the National Interagency Coordination Center is light.

For contractors and businesses that operate around shared fire resources, that is a useful seasonal operating signal: the national system has moved away from peak competition for crews and equipment even though local restrictions, incidents and contract needs still vary by region.

Mills, Markets & Procurement

SOUTH — GEORGIA

Georgia picks Langdale for an $8.9 million forest-products pilot plant

The Georgia Forestry Innovation Initiative has selected The Langdale Company as industry collaborator for a pilot-scale manufacturing facility in Valdosta. The $8.9 million state-funded initiative is intended to move technologies from laboratory work toward commercial deployment using underutilized woody biomass, forestry residuals and mill byproducts as feedstocks for higher-value chemicals, advanced materials, carbon products, fuels and other forest-derived products.

The useful business signal is the pilot scale. Instead of funding another study of low-grade wood, Georgia is pairing research capacity with an established forest-products operator and a physical site where technologies can be tested under operating conditions before larger capital commitments are made.

Watch: Planning, engineering, permitting and site-development work begins now; the stated goal is to start construction before year-end and move toward operational readiness in 2027.

Publications & Tools

NATIONAL — FOREST MARKETS / TECHNOLOGY

Two funded tools aim directly at the forest-to-mill information gap

The U.S. Endowment for Forestry and Communities has awarded $500,000 for two forest-technology projects. CTrees receives $300,000 to build Forest Asset Intelligence: The Wood Triangle, combining forest conditions and ownership with mill capacity, procurement and logistics, demand, product specifications and certification. Spatial Informatics Group receives $200,000 for a Planscape Bioeconomy Module that will estimate material from forest-health treatments and compare harvest and transportation costs with potential market value.

Both projects are aimed at a problem forest businesses know well: useful supply, treatment, haul and market information often exists in separate systems. The Endowment says both tools now move into development and testing for use across regions, ownership types and market conditions.

WEST — CALIFORNIA

CAL FIRE viewer puts proposed and approved harvest activity on one map

CAL FIRE's public Forest Practice Viewer brings proposed and approved timber-harvest activity into a searchable map, including Timber Harvesting Plans, Emergency and Exemption Notices, Nonindustrial Timber Management Plans, Working Forest Management Plans and related notices. Users can add other data layers and create GeoPDFs, with forest-practice roads and hydrology available alongside harvest information.

For operators, consultants and landowners, the practical value is situational awareness: nearby planned activity, road context and watershed information can be checked in one place rather than assembled from separate filings and maps.

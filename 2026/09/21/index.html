Markets & Pricing

NATIONAL — OPERATING COSTS

Diesel and lumber are moving in opposite directions

The operating-cost side of the wood business tightened again last week while the product-price side weakened. The EIA's national on-highway diesel benchmark reached $6.285 per gallon for the week of September 14. Meanwhile, November lumber futures closed Friday at $537 per thousand board feet after settling at $578.50 on September 11.

That spread is the business signal: logging, trucking and mill-delivery costs are being pushed higher by fuel at the same time the most visible softwood benchmark has moved lower. For businesses without effective fuel pass-throughs, the squeeze can show up in contribution margin before it is obvious in gross revenue.

Watch: EIA's next weekly diesel release is due September 22; it will show whether the latest fuel spike is still accelerating.

Under the Radar

NATIONAL — EQUIPMENT / MAINTENANCE

Base-oil shortage is pushing another cost line higher

A shortage of Group III base oils used in synthetic lubricants is beginning to reach U.S. retail and service channels. Reporting last week put retail motor-oil prices up 24.2% between February and August, with some oil-change prices already moving $5 to $7 higher.

For forestry and forest-products businesses, this is smaller than diesel but harder to avoid: loaders, skidders, trucks, pickups and mill mobile equipment all consume lubricants. A prolonged shortage turns a routine maintenance input into another place where purchasing discipline and inventory visibility matter.

Publications & Tools

NATIONAL — FOREST MANAGEMENT

Free platform combines wildfire, drought and pest risk for land managers

American Forests has launched its Forest Innovation Platform, a free mapping and decision-support tool intended to bring wildfire, drought, pest and other forest-risk information together across private, state, Tribal, federal and NGO lands.

The useful part is not another map by itself; it is the attempt to put multiple risk layers into one operating view. For consulting foresters, land managers and businesses evaluating treatment needs, a common platform can reduce the time spent assembling separate datasets before deciding where field work deserves attention.

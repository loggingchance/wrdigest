NATIONAL — TAX CREDITS / NEW RESOURCE

New searchable sourcebook maps tax credits for forest-products businesses

tax.lumbermen.org has launched as a searchable web resource built around the National Forest Products Tax Credit Sourcebook 2026 Edition. The publication was compiled by Steve Bick, who hosts The Woods Run Digest through The Forest Business School, together with Alison Berry and Maura Ross.

The resource helps forest-products businesses find federal and state tax-credit opportunities and assess practical applicability across mills, logging companies, wood-energy businesses, manufacturers and other forest-products operations. The complete PDF can be downloaded from the site.

Market Opportunities

Southeast — South Carolina

Sand Hills sale offers about 8,100 tons with all-weather operability

The South Carolina Forestry Commission will open bids September 15 at 10 a.m. on sale SHSF-5367-ADV-TS-27 in Darlington County. The prospectus estimates roughly 7,900 tons of pine and 200 tons of hardwood on about 79 acres, with a 12-month operating period and all-weather logging.

Bids are structured as one blended price per ton for pine, one for hardwood and one for chip products. The successful buyer must provide a $5,000 performance bond and $25,000 advance; the sale calls for a clearcut of all merchantable forest products.

Government / Funding / Trade

Northeast — Maine

$60 million disaster program includes timber losses and forester costs

Maine’s Natural Resources Disaster Relief Assistance Program is accepting applications through November 6. The state says forestry businesses and other eligible producers may seek assistance for qualifying 2023–24 severe-weather losses, including standing timber and harvested timber.

For timber claims, applicants who pass the initial eligibility review must hire a licensed forester to complete additional documentation. Paid invoices for that forester are themselves eligible for reimbursement, an important detail for landowners deciding whether a claim is worth developing.

Canada — Quebec

Quebec wood-innovation funding closes Friday

Quebec has reopened Programme Innovation Bois, with the current call for projects due September 18. Eligible lanes include investment-oriented innovation, forest biomass and lower-quality wood utilization, and projects intended to accelerate value-added production.

For mills and wood-product companies, the deadline is the immediate issue: businesses with a project already defined have only days left in this intake. A separate study-oriented innovation stream is expected to remain continuously open in winter 2027, subject to available funding.

Forestry & Timber

Pacific Northwest — Oregon

Oregon enters its September state-timber bid week

Oregon Department of Forestry’s September bid-opening window runs September 14–18. ODF sells nearly all state-forest timber through competitive bidding and publishes monthly sale schedules and subsequent results.

The useful signal this week will be the spread between appraised values and winning bids across individual sales. With western mill demand uneven, the results can help show where procurement competition remains strongest by operating area and species mix.

New Publications, Data & Tools

Canada’s weekly forest-product price page consolidates lumber, panels and pulp

Canada · Market data · Current update September 9, 2026

Natural Resources Canada’s current-price page combines weekly North American softwood lumber and panel pricing with monthly pulp prices in the United States, Europe and China and exchange-rate data for Canada’s major trading partners.

It is less granular than a paid market service, but useful as a consistent public benchmark—particularly for readers who want one government-maintained place to check broad lumber, panel and pulp direction without mixing data from several sources.

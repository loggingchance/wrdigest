Latest edition

September 5, 2026

Daily edition
  • Washington State raised the no-board-approval threshold for salvage sales from $250,000 to $2 million after record fire damage to state trust forests.
  • After the Coosa Pines, Alabama pulp mill and associated chip facility stopped buying, pine pulpwood stumpage reportedly fell from about $7/ton to effectively zero within an 85-mile radius.
  • Tahoe Forest Products secured $50 million in USDA-backed financing to expand Sierra Nevada sawmilling capacity tied to forest-restoration material.
  • Madison’s Lumber Prices Index ended the week at $518/MBF, down about 1% from the prior week.
  • Contractor opportunities include 2,520 green tons of biomass removal at French Meadows, California, and a Forest Service thinning/hand-piling job in eastern Oregon.

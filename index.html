Daily edition
  • LP Building Solutions will indefinitely curtail its Jasper, Texas OSB mill, removing roughly 475 million square feet of annual 3/8-inch-basis capacity; about 150 jobs are affected.
  • A 573-driver Southern log-trucking study identifies experience requirements, insurance qualification and startup capital as the main barriers to third-party recruitment.
  • A 1,119.7-acre timber tract near Astoria, Oregon is asking $5.7 million; more than 90% is listed as Site Class I or II.
  • ForestSource's refreshed slab dataset contains 24,324 observations from 94 dealers; black walnut asking prices have a $18/BF median.
  • New practical tools cover forest-road runoff/erosion and real-time slash-pile volume estimation on LiDAR-equipped iPhones and iPads.

September 9, 2026

Daily edition
  • Fuel is becoming an immediate logging-margin problem as the American Loggers Council says many wood haulers are not receiving fuel surcharges comparable to other freight carriers.
  • Washington DNR has issued active-management guidance after the state's first confirmed emerald ash borer detection, pushing managers to inventory ash and plan treatment, removal and replacement before decline.
  • Washington DNR's current unsold list includes 4,960 MBF at Q Parody Peak and 5,238 MBF at Country Boy, both still available at minimum appraised value.
  • A U.S. Army Corps pay-as-cut timber sale at Red River Army Depot closes September 10 with an estimated 12,200 tons of pine and hardwood products.
  • Contract Lumber has acquired HT Building Products in Houston and Dallas, expanding a major lumber and building-products supplier deeper into Texas and the Southwest.
